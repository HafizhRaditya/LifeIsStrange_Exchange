Plates

Every background in the season, built as SVG — no photography. Two were drawn by hand (lighthouse-dusk, pool-night); the rest are composed from shared archetypes by tools/make_plates.py, so they hold one light language and can be regenerated when the palette moves. Swapping any of them for real art means changing a url() in css/plates.css — no JS knows they exist.
